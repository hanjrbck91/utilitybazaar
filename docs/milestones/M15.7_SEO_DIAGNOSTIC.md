**Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Production at audit time:** `3840520` (M15.6)
**Question:** UtilityBazaar got some Google impressions after more calculators
launched, then fell back toward zero. Why?

Every conclusion carries a label:
**[CONFIRMED]** directly supported by data · **[LIKELY]** strong inference
from several signals · **[HYPOTHESIS]** plausible, not proven ·
**[UNKNOWN]** not enough evidence.

Evidence sources used (all collected 2026-10-08):
- Production HTML/HTTP fetched directly from `https://utilitybazaar.in`.
- Live Google India SERPs (`google.co.in`, desktop, `hl=en`, unpersonalized;
  the plain "gst calculator" query returned no data and is not counted)
  through the Apify Google Search Results Scraper: 15 queries × page 1,
  5 head queries × pages 1–5, 4 `site:`/mention queries.
- Google autocomplete (`gl=in`) for query variants.
- RDAP (domain registration), Wayback Machine CDX (domain history),
  Common Crawl index.
- Competitor pages fetched directly (title, word count, headings, schema,
  internal links).
- Git history of this repo.

**Not available:** Google Search Console. No search-volume tool (no Google
Ads Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Semrush). No backlink index.

## 1. Executive summary

The site is **not technically broken** and **is indexed**. It **does not
rank** for any head query: it is absent from the top ~40–50 Google India
results for all five head terms checked. The domain is **41 days old**
(registered 2026-08-28), has **no visible external mentions or links**,
and its brand query ("utilitybazaar") returns unrelated Brazilian shops.

So the funnel breaks at **Ranking**, not Discovery or Indexing:

```
Discovery  ✅ [CONFIRMED]  salary pages indexed ≤1 day after release
Indexing   ✅ [CONFIRMED]  10 of 11 sitemap URLs seen in Google; /en not observed [UNKNOWN]
Ranking    ❌ [CONFIRMED]  not in top ~40–50 for any head term checked
Impressions ↓ (reported by owner; Search Console not accessed)
Clicks      ~0 (follows from the above)
```

The rise-then-fall pattern is best explained by **[LIKELY]** normal
new-domain behaviour: Google briefly tests new pages, then settles them
low because the domain has no authority signals, in SERPs dominated by
fintechs, tax portals and exact-match calculator domains. The
2026-09-18 restructure (redirects reversed, site renamed) **[HYPOTHESIS]**
may have added churn at the same time.

The single most valuable next step is **data, not changes**: connect or
export Search Console so the query/page/date evidence replaces inference.

## 12. Probable causes of the rise-then-fall

1. **[LIKELY]** New-domain volatility. New pages often get brief test
   impressions at varied positions, then settle low when there are no
   trust signals. This fits the timing: the domain was registered
   2026-08-28, Percentage launched 2026-09-12, and impressions rose and
   then fell.
2. **[LIKELY]** Competition. The head terms are owned by high-authority
   fintech, tax and government sites plus older exact-match domains.
   That's a ranking and competition problem, not an indexing one.
3. **[LIKELY]** Any early impressions probably came from long-tail or
   unusual queries at deep positions. They're easy to gain and easy to
   lose. (Can't be confirmed without the query data.)

## 13. Hypotheses that remain unproven

- **[HYPOTHESIS]** The 2026-09-18 restructure (M14.1) contributed. `/`
  changed from a 308 to `/en/gst-calculator` into a 308 to `/en`. The 308s
  `/en`→`/en/gst-calculator` and `/hi`→`/hi/gst-calculator` were removed,
  so `/en` and `/hi` became pages. The site name changed from "GST
  Calculator" to "UtilityBazaar". Reversing permanent redirects and
  renaming at once can cause re-evaluation churn. Test: compare the
  Search Console daily impressions around 2026-09-18.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** The previous owner's domain history (unrelated
  e-commerce, possible spam or penalty history) is suppressing trust.
  Test: Search Console → Manual actions and Security issues.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** The Hindi pages miss demand because users search in
  Hinglish (Latin script). Test: Search Console queries for `/hi/*` pages.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** `/en` is not indexed. Test: URL Inspection.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** Long-tail pages (e.g. "in hand salary for 12 lpa",
  "18% GST on 25000") could earn impressions sooner than head terms.
  Unproven, and carries thin-content risk if done carelessly.

4. **Release the M15.7 navigation commit** (`3c38736`) through the normal

5. **Freeze URLs, titles and structure** for 4–6 weeks while the

## 15. Actions explicitly NOT recommended yet

- Rewriting titles, descriptions or H1s, or adding keywords ("India",
  "2026", "free", "online") without query data.
- Adding schema types (BreadcrumbList, HowTo, Organization) as a
  ranking fix. Pages with no schema outrank ours (§7).
- Inflating content length. ~17- and ~310-word pages rank #1 (§7).
- Buying links, link exchanges, directory submissions, or SEO services.
- Building more calculators *as an SEO tactic*.
- Mass-generating "X lpa in hand" or "GST on ₹N" pages before demand and
  quality are validated.
- Changing URLs, locale structure or hreflang. They're correct, and
  changing them creates exactly the churn we suspect hurt in September.
- Rebranding. The brand collision is real, but it's a business decision,
  not an SEO quick fix.

## 17. Proposed SEO strategy for the next milestone

**Principle:** measure first. Then compete where a new domain can win,
and build trust slowly. Don't optimize the head terms we can't win yet.

1. **M15.8 (proposed) — Measurement baseline.** Connect Search Console,
   pull the §16 data, and record a dated baseline in this doc. Recheck the
   §6 SERPs. Decide from evidence which of the §13 hypotheses are real.
2. **Then, only if the data supports it**, run one small, reversible
   experiment at a time. Candidates, in order of expected leverage for a
   new domain:
   - a. Hinglish phrasing on the Hindi pages, *if* `/hi` queries show
     Latin-script demand.
   - b. One carefully built long-tail cluster where small sites already
     rank (e.g. reverse GST, or CTC-to-in-hand for common CTC amounts),
     with genuinely useful content and no thin pages.
   - c. Contextual links from About to the tools (low risk).
3. **Trust building (non-SEO-service):** organic mentions come from real
   use, for example sharing the tools where Indian users ask these
   questions. Track brand impressions in Search Console as the signal.
4. **Expectation setting:** for a two-month-old domain against Groww,
   ClearTax and AmbitionBox, head-term rankings are a months-long
   outcome. The near-term goal is long-tail impressions that grow
   steadily, not head-term positions.
