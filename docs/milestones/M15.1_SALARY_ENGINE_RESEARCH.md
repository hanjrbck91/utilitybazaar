# M15.1 — Salary Engine: Research & Specification

**Status:** ✅ complete 2026-10-07 · specification locked before coding · results in §10
**Scope:** the pure engine only (`src/lib/salary/`). No UI, routes, translations or SEO.

## 1. User problem
People search "salary calculator", "CTC to in-hand" and "take-home salary"
because an Indian offer letter states **CTC**, and CTC is not what reaches
the bank account. CTC includes money the employee never receives monthly:
the employer's PF share, variable pay and gratuity. The employee's own PF,
professional tax and income tax (TDS) are deducted from what remains. The
job is:

> "My CTC is X. How much will actually reach my bank account every month?"

## 2. Market reference model (product behaviour, not legal authority)
Checked on 2026-10-07.

| Product | What we borrow |
|---|---|
| **ClearTax** (cleartax.in/s/salary-calculator) | The overall flow: **Annual CTC → variable pay → deductions → income tax (new regime) → annual and monthly take-home**. Its inputs are Annual CTC, Bonus/Variable %, and optional monthly employer PF, employee PF, annual professional tax and other deductions. |
| **Groww** (groww.in/calculators/salary-calculator) | The simple breakdown: CTC, "bonus included in CTC %", and monthly professional tax and PF lines, with **total deductions** plus **monthly and annual take-home**. It has no income tax. Its PF defaults (₹1,800) still assume the old ₹15,000 ceiling. |
| **AmbitionBox** (ambitionbox.com/salaries/take-home-salary-calculator) | The entry point: **Annual CTC first**, with only bonus and tax regime beside it. A result needs no salary-structure knowledge. |

## 3. V1 inputs
| Input | Required | Default | Range | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `annualCtc` (₹) | **yes** | — | > 0, ≤ ₹10 crore | The one thing every offer letter states |
| `variablePayPercent` (% of CTC) | no | 0 | 0 to < 100 | Every market calculator has it; variable pay is not monthly cash |
| `pfWagesPercent` (Basic + DA as % of fixed CTC) | no | **50** | 1–100 | PF and gratuity depend on it, but CTC alone can't give it. **Estimation default**, see §6 |
| `pfMode` | no | `"full"` | `full` · `capped` · `none` | Employers either contribute on full wages or limit to the ₹25,000 ceiling |
| `includeGratuity` | no | `false` | boolean | Some CTCs include a gratuity provision |
| `professionalTaxAnnual` (₹) | no | 0 | 0–2,500 | State-specific; the user enters it |

## 4. V1 outputs
The engine returns everything below, so the UI can explain *"your take-home is X because…"*.
- **Annual:** CTC, variable pay, fixed CTC, employer PF, gratuity provision,
  gross salary (cash, including variable pay), employee PF, professional
  tax, income tax, **take-home**.
- **Monthly (regular month, without variable pay):** PF wages, employer
  PF, employee PF, gross salary, professional tax, income tax (TDS),
  **take-home**.
- **Tax working:** gross salary, standard deduction, taxable income, a
  slab-by-slab tax table, rebate, surcharge, marginal relief, cess, total.
- **Flags:** PF limited by the ceiling, wages below the 50% floor,
  rebate applied, rebate marginal relief applied, surcharge relief applied.

## 6. Simplifications (not statutory facts)
1. **Employer PF is inside CTC.** This is a market convention: ClearTax's
   formula "CTC = Basic + Allowances + Perks + Employer PF + Bonus".
   No statute requires it.
2. **PF wages (Basic + DA) = `pfWagesPercent`% of fixed CTC, default 50%.**
   The real figure is on the payslip. 50% follows the statutory wage floor,
   but the floor is defined on "remuneration", not on fixed CTC. So this is
   an **estimate**, and the engine reports `wagesBelowStatutoryFloor` when
   the user enters less than 50%.
3. **Employer PF equals employee PF** (12% each, same base). EDLI and admin
   charges, which some CTCs include, are not modelled.
4. **Gratuity is a provision** (monthly wages × 15/26 per year) that is in
   CTC but never paid monthly. It is off by default because the market
   calculators don't model it.
5. **Variable pay is assumed to be paid in full within the tax year.** It is
   taxable, and appears in **annual** take-home only. The monthly take-home
   is a regular month.
6. **Income tax (TDS) is spread evenly over 12 months.** Real payroll
   re-estimates during the year.
7. **Professional tax is one annual amount**, shown monthly as ÷12. Several
   states charge a different amount in one month (e.g. Feb), so ÷12 is
   an average.
8. **Resident individual, salary income only, new regime only.** No other
   income, no employer-NPS deduction, no HRA or old-regime deductions.
9. **Rounding:** CTC, variable pay and gratuity are rounded to the nearest
   rupee. PF is rounded to the nearest rupee per month. Slab tax,
   surcharge and cess are rounded to the nearest rupee (half-up). Monthly
   figures derived by ÷12 keep paise (2 dp). The tax rounding rules in the
   Act are not modelled.
10. **Employer contributions above ₹7.5 lakh/year** (PF + NPS +
    superannuation, taxable as a perquisite) are not modelled. They are
    reachable only with very high wages.

## 7. Out of scope (V1)
Salary comparison, cost of living, negotiation advice, ESOPs, retirement
planning, offer comparison, salary hike calculator, freelance income, NRI
salary, old tax regime, HRA/80C/investment planning, ESI, labour welfare
fund, state professional-tax tables, reverse (in-hand → CTC) mode.

## 8. Engine model
```
variablePay      = round(ctc × variablePayPercent / 100)
fixedCtc         = ctc − variablePay
pfWagesMonthly   = fixedCtc × pfWagesPercent / 100 / 12
pfBaseMonthly    = full: pfWagesMonthly · capped: min(pfWagesMonthly, 25000) · none: 0
employeePfMonthly= round(0.12 × pfBaseMonthly)     employerPfMonthly = same
gratuityAnnual   = includeGratuity ? round(pfWagesMonthly × 15 / 26) : 0
fixedGrossAnnual = fixedCtc − 12·employerPfMonthly − gratuityAnnual
grossSalary      = fixedGrossAnnual + variablePay
taxable          = grossSalary − min(75000, grossSalary)
slabTax          = Σ slabs                         (rounded)
rebate           = s.156(2): ≤12L → min(slabTax, 60000); >12L → max(0, slabTax − (taxable − 12L))
surcharge        = band rate × (slabTax − rebate), capped by marginal relief at the band threshold
incomeTax        = (slabTax − rebate + surcharge) × 1.04   (cess rounded)
annualTakeHome   = grossSalary − 12·employeePfMonthly − professionalTax − incomeTax
monthlyTakeHome  = (fixedGrossAnnual − 12·employeePfMonthly − professionalTax − incomeTax) / 12
```
Accounting identities (tested):
- `ctc = fixedGrossAnnual + variablePay + employerPfAnnual + gratuityAnnual`
- `grossSalary − employeePfAnnual − professionalTax − incomeTax = annualTakeHome`
- `monthlyTakeHome × 12 + variablePay ≈ annualTakeHome` (within 6 paise of rounding)

## 9. Market comparison (engine vs reference products)
| Scenario | Reference | Ours | Why they differ |
|---|---|---|---|
| ClearTax default (₹15L fixed + 30% bonus = ₹19.5L, no PF/PT, new regime) | tax ₹1,82,000 · annual take-home ₹17,68,000 · "monthly" ₹1,47,333 | tax **₹1,82,000** · annual **₹17,68,000** · regular month ₹1,09,833.33 | Tax and annual figures match exactly. ClearTax's monthly figure is annual ÷ 12 with the bonus averaged in; ours is a month without the bonus, which is what actually reaches the account each month |
| Groww default (₹6L, 15% bonus, PT ₹200/m, PF ₹1,800/m typed in, no tax) | ₹38,700/month | ₹37,200/month (capped mode) | Groww uses the PF the user types; its default ₹1,800 assumes the **old ₹15,000 ceiling**. We estimate PF from wages (₹2,550). Groww has no income tax |

Same journey as the market (**CTC → components → deductions → tax →
take-home**), with three deliberate differences:
- PF is estimated from wages rather than typed in, and uses the current
  ₹25,000 ceiling.
- Professional tax doesn't reduce taxable income under the new regime.
- Monthly take-home excludes variable pay.

- **One finding during verification:** inside marginal-relief zones
  (just above ₹12 lakh, and ₹50 lakh / ₹1 crore / ₹2 crore), take-home can
  fall slightly as CTC rises. The cess has **no marginal relief** (Budget
  2026 Memorandum), so each extra rupee there costs ₹1.04. This is correct
  law, not a bug, and is covered by a test.

## 11. Known limitations
- Income tax uses the new regime only, for TY 2026-27. **Slabs, rebate,
  standard deduction, surcharge, cess and the PF ceiling must be re-checked
  after each Budget or EPFO notification** (all in `constants.ts`).
- PF wages are estimated (default 50% of fixed CTC). Real payslips differ.
- Employer PF is assumed to be inside CTC. EDLI and admin charges are
  ignored.
- TDS is spread evenly over the year. Real payroll re-estimates.
- Professional tax is an annual amount averaged over 12 months.
- Not modelled: old regime, HRA/80C, employer NPS, ESI, other income,
  non-residents, perquisites, and the ₹7.5 lakh employer-contribution limit.
